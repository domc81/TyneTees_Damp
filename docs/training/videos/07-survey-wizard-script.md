# Voiceover Script — The Survey Wizard (17s)

**[0:00]** The survey wizard is the tool surveyors use on-site. It has five steps, shown at the top of the screen.

**[0:03]** Step One captures site details — the property photo, date, weather, and property type.

**[0:05]** Step Two is the external inspection — walk around the outside and record any defects.

**[0:08]** Step Three is room-by-room inspection. Add each room, tick what issues you find, take photos, and record observations. This is the main part of the survey.

**[0:12]** Step Four covers whole-property items like ventilation units, skips, and travel distance.

**[0:14]** Step Five is the review — check everything looks right, then click Complete Survey. The system automatically calculates the costing.
