# Voiceover Scripts for Training Videos

Read each script aloud while playing the corresponding video. Timestamps are approximate — adjust your pacing to match what's on screen. Speak slowly and clearly.

---

## Video 01 — Logging In (10s)

**[0:00]** To log in, go to the website address your administrator has given you. You'll see the login screen with the company logo.

**[0:03]** Type your email address into the Email box.

**[0:05]** Then type your password into the Password box.

**[0:07]** Click the blue Sign In button. After a moment, you'll be taken to your Dashboard — your home screen.

---

## Video 02 — Sidebar Navigation (10s)

**[0:00]** The sidebar on the left is how you get around the system. Let's click through each section.

**[0:02]** Surveys shows all your survey projects as cards.

**[0:03]** Customers lists everyone in the system with their contact details.

**[0:05]** Enquiries is the pipeline board — this is where office staff manage new leads.

**[0:06]** Calendar shows all the survey bookings.

**[0:07]** Training is where you'll find these how-to guides.

**[0:08]** Settings is for system configuration. And Dashboard brings you back home.

---

## Video 03 — Creating an Enquiry (10s)

**[0:00]** When a customer phones in, you create a new enquiry. Start on the Enquiries page and click the New Enquiry button.

**[0:03]** The form has three steps. In Step One, enter the client's name — that's the only required field. Add their email and phone number if you have them.

**[0:07]** Step Two asks for the property address and survey type. Step Three lets you assign a surveyor and pick a date. Click Create Enquiry when you're done, and it appears in the New column on the pipeline board.

## Video 04 — The Enquiry Pipeline (12s)

**[0:00]** The enquiry pipeline is a Kanban board. Each column represents a stage — New, Assigned, Surveyed, Quoted, and so on.

**[0:04]** Click on any card to see the full details in a side panel. You can edit fields directly from here — change the priority, assign a surveyor, or add notes.

**[0:08]** Use the toggle at the top to show declined and on-hold enquiries. The search box lets you find specific enquiries by name, address, or reference number.

---

## Video 05 — Managing Customers (6s)

**[0:00]** The Customers page lists everyone in the system. Use the search box to quickly find someone by name, email, phone, or postcode.

**[0:03]** Click on any customer to see their full details — contact information, survey history, and a log of all communications.

---

## Video 06 — Viewing a Survey (6s)

**[0:00]** From the Surveys page, click on any survey card to see its detail page.

**[0:02]** This shows you everything in one place — the customer details, the property address, the survey appointment, and the current status.

**[0:04]** Scroll down to see the quotation information and the action buttons — Continue Survey, View Costing, View Report, and Installer Info.

---

## Video 07 — The Survey Wizard (17s)

**[0:00]** The survey wizard is the tool surveyors use on-site. It has five steps, shown at the top of the screen.

**[0:03]** Step One captures site details — the property photo, date, weather, and property type.

**[0:05]** Step Two is the external inspection — walk around the outside and record any defects.

**[0:08]** Step Three is room-by-room inspection. Add each room, tick what issues you find, take photos, and record observations. This is the main part of the survey.

**[0:12]** Step Four covers whole-property items like ventilation units, skips, and travel distance.

**[0:14]** Step Five is the review — check everything looks right, then click Complete Survey. The system automatically calculates the costing.

## Video 08 — Survey Costing (7s)

**[0:00]** The costing page shows you exactly how the job has been priced. At the top, you'll see the project overheads — travel costs and vehicle mileage.

**[0:03]** Below that is the job cost summary — mandatory works, optional works, subtotal, VAT, and the grand total.

**[0:05]** Scroll down to see each section broken out line by line — material costs, labour hours, and line totals. The system calculates all of this automatically from the survey data.

## Video 09 — Using the Calendar (15s)

**[0:00]** The calendar shows all survey bookings across all surveyors. Each surveyor has their own colour.

**[0:03]** Use the filter buttons at the top to show or hide individual surveyors. Click All to see everyone.

**[0:06]** Switch between Day, Week, and Month views using the buttons in the top right. Day view is useful for seeing the detail, while Month gives you the big picture.

**[0:11]** Click on any booking to see the details — customer name, address, survey type, and status. From here you can confirm, reschedule, or cancel the appointment.

**[0:00]** The Team page lists all user accounts. You can see each person's name, email, role, whether they're a surveyor, and their current status.

**[0:03]** Use the search box to find someone quickly, or filter by role. Click the edit icon to change someone's details, or click Add Team Member to create a new account.

## Video 11 — Pricing Configuration (7s)

**[0:00]** The Pricing Configuration page is where you set the rates that feed into every costing calculation.

**[0:02]** At the top are the labour rates and markups. Below that, you'll find the fixed costs like skip hire and asbestos testing.

**[0:04]** Further down are the deposit percentages for each survey type, and the survey fee amount.

**[0:00]** The availability page lets you manage each surveyor's working hours. Select a surveyor from the dropdown at the top.

**[0:04]** Below that, you can see and manage absence blocks — holidays, sickness, and training days. Click Add Absence to book time off.

**[0:00]** Click Training in the sidebar to find all the how-to guides. The hub page shows four guides, with recommendations based on your role.

**[0:04]** Click into any guide to read it. Each guide has a table of contents on the right — click any section to jump straight to it.

**[0:07]** The guides include screenshots from the actual system, tips, and step-by-step instructions. Click any screenshot to see it full-size.

## Recording Tips

- **Pace:** Aim for a calm, steady pace. These are for people who may be nervous about new technology.
- **Tone:** Friendly and reassuring, not robotic. Imagine you're showing a colleague over their shoulder.
- **Pauses:** Leave a beat between sections — the viewer needs time to look at the screen.
- **Re-takes:** Don't worry about getting it perfect first time. Record each script separately so you can redo individual clips.
- **Equipment:** A decent microphone makes a big difference. Even a phone headset is better than a laptop mic.
- **Editing:** Import each MP4 into your video editor (iMovie, DaVinci Resolve, CapCut, etc.), add the audio track, and export. The videos have no sound, so your voiceover is the only audio.
