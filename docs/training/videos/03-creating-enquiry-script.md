# Voiceover Script — Creating an Enquiry (10s)

**[0:00]** When a customer phones in, you create a new enquiry. Start on the Enquiries page and click the New Enquiry button.

**[0:03]** The form has three steps. In Step One, enter the client's name — that's the only required field. Add their email and phone number if you have them.

**[0:07]** Step Two asks for the property address and survey type. Step Three lets you assign a surveyor and pick a date. Click Create Enquiry when you're done, and it appears in the New column on the pipeline board.
